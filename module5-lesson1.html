😌
Stay calm and assess the situation
Panic can make things worse. Take a moment to assess what's happening so you can respond safely and effectively.
🚨
Raise the alarm
Alert others immediately by activating the nearest alarm system or notifying the appropriate person in charge.
🚪
Follow evacuation routes
Use the designated emergency exits and avoid using lifts. Always follow the direction of fire wardens or emergency staff.
🤝
Assist others if safe to do so
Help colleagues, visitors, or anyone who may need support, but never put yourself in danger.
📍
Report to the assembly point
Once outside, go directly to the designated assembly point and wait until given further instructions or clearance to return.
