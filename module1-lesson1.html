Lesson 1: What "Health and Safety" Means and Why It Matters

Protecting people from harm

Health and safety means creating safe working conditions that prevent injury, illness, and accidents for employees, visitors, and contractors.

Ensuring everyone goes home safe

The goal of workplace safety is simple — every person should end their day in the same good health as when they started, free from avoidable harm.

Preventing accidents and illness

By identifying risks early and following safe practices, organisations can reduce workplace incidents and long-term health problems caused by unsafe environments.

Building trust and productivity

A safe workplace shows employees that their wellbeing matters. When people feel secure, morale and productivity improve naturally.

Legal and shared responsibility

Health and safety is both a legal requirement and a shared duty — employers must provide safe systems, and employees must follow them to keep everyone protected.
