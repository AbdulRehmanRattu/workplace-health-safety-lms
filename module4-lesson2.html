Module 4: Safe Working Practices and Equipment Use

Lesson 2: Safe Use of Tools, Machinery, and PPE

🔍
Inspect before use
Always check tools and machinery for damage, wear, or defects before starting work to prevent accidents or malfunctions.
📖
Follow manufacturer instructions
Use equipment only as intended and in line with the manufacturer's safety guidelines and operational manuals.
🛡️
Keep guards and safety devices in place
Never remove or bypass protective guards, switches, or interlocks — they are there to prevent serious injury.
🦺
Use the correct PPE for the task
Wear appropriate personal protective equipment such as gloves, goggles, helmets, or ear protection depending on the job.
⚠️
Report faults immediately
If equipment is damaged, faulty, or unsafe, stop using it right away and report it so it can be repaired or replaced safely.
