Lesson 1: Manual Handling and Preventing Slips, Trips, and Falls

📦 Understanding manual handling
Manual handling includes lifting, carrying, pushing, or pulling loads. Poor technique can lead to strains, sprains, or long-term back injuries.
🏋️ Safe lifting techniques
Plan the lift, bend your knees (not your back), keep the load close to your body, and avoid twisting while carrying heavy items.
⚠️ Preventing slips, trips, and falls
Keep floors clear, clean up spills immediately, and ensure cables or tools are stored properly to avoid accidents.
🚛 Using the right equipment
Use mechanical aids like trolleys or lifting devices where possible. Never lift more than you can handle safely.
📢 Reporting hazards promptly
If you notice uneven floors, loose mats, or poor lighting, report it straight away to prevent others from getting hurt.
