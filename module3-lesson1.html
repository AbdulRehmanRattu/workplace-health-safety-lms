A risk assessment is a careful examination of what could cause harm in the workplace, so you can weigh up precautions to prevent it.
They help protect people, reduce accidents, and ensure legal compliance by identifying hazards before they cause harm.
Employers are legally responsible for carrying out risk assessments, but everyone should play a part by reporting hazards and unsafe conditions.
Assessments should be done regularly, especially when new equipment, processes, or staff changes occur.
All findings should be documented, shared with relevant staff, and reviewed periodically to ensure control measures remain effective.
