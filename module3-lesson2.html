🔍
Step 1: Identify hazards
Look around the workplace and consider what could cause harm — such as equipment, materials, tasks, or environmental factors.
👥
Step 2: Decide who might be harmed and how
Think about employees, visitors, contractors, and anyone else who could be affected by the hazards identified.
🛡️
Step 3: Evaluate the risks and decide on precautions
Assess how likely harm is to occur and determine what safety measures are needed to reduce or eliminate the risk.
📋
Step 4: Record your findings
Document what you've found, including hazards, risks, and control measures, and make sure this information is shared with your team.
🔄
Step 5: Review and update regularly
Regularly check that safety measures are still effective, and update your risk assessment whenever circumstances change.
