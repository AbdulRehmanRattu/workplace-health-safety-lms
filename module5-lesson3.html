Module 5: Emergency Procedures and Incident Reporting

Lesson 3: The Importance of Accurate Records and Follow-Up Actions

📄
Why accurate records matter
Detailed records provide evidence of incidents, support investigations, and help organisations comply with legal requirements.
📊
Tracking and analysing trends
Recording all incidents, including near misses, helps identify patterns and recurring hazards that may need further attention.
📋
Ensuring accountability
Accurate documentation clarifies who was involved, what actions were taken, and whether proper safety procedures were followed.
⚙️
Driving continuous improvement
Follow-up reviews after incidents help strengthen safety systems, update training, and prevent similar events in the future.
🤝
Building organisational trust
Transparent record-keeping demonstrates a commitment to safety and encourages employees to report concerns responsibly.
