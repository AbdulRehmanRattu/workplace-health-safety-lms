Accidents, injuries, unsafe conditions, and near misses should all be reported, even if no one was seriously harmed.
Follow your organisation's reporting procedure or complete the relevant incident form as soon as possible after the event.
Include the date, time, location, people involved, and a brief description of what happened to help the investigation.
Report directly to your line manager or the person responsible for health and safety so appropriate action can be taken.
Every report helps identify risks and prevent future accidents. Sharing lessons learned promotes a safer workplace for everyone.
