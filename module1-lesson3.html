Lesson 3: The Importance of a Positive Safety Culture

🏢
Understanding safety culture
A safety culture reflects how seriously an organisation values health and safety — it's seen in attitudes, behaviours, and everyday decisions at all levels.
👥
Leading by example
Managers and supervisors set the tone by demonstrating safe behaviour, encouraging open communication, and responding promptly to safety concerns.
📝
Encouraging reporting and feedback
A strong safety culture allows employees to report hazards, near misses, or unsafe practices without fear of blame or punishment.
🏆
Recognising and rewarding safe behaviour
Positive reinforcement, such as praising or rewarding safe actions, helps normalise safety as part of everyday work habits.
📈
Continuous improvement mindset
Safety culture isn't static — it grows stronger through learning from incidents, updating processes, and maintaining open discussions about safety.
