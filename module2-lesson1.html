Lesson 1: Common Workplace Hazards (Physical, Chemical, Ergonomic, Biological, and Psychosocial)

⚡ Physical hazards
These include slips, trips, falls, noise, vibration, and unsafe machinery. They are the most common workplace hazards and can cause immediate injury if not controlled.
🧪 Chemical hazards
Exposure to cleaning agents, fumes, or toxic substances can harm skin, eyes, or lungs. Safe storage, labelling, and handling procedures reduce these risks.
💺 Ergonomic hazards
Poor posture, repetitive movements, or inadequate workstation design can cause strain, fatigue, and musculoskeletal problems over time.
🦠 Biological hazards
Viruses, bacteria, mould, or other microorganisms can spread illness in certain workplaces, especially healthcare, cleaning, or food industries.
🧠 Psychosocial hazards
Stress, harassment, long hours, or poor communication can negatively impact mental health and performance. These are just as important to address as physical risks.
