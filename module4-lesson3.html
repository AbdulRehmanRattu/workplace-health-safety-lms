Understanding the fire triangle

Never take unnecessary risks
🔥 Click on each fire safety step to learn the basics
Fire needs three elements to ignite — heat, fuel, and oxygen. Removing any one of these will prevent or extinguish a fire.
Familiarise yourself with the nearest fire exits and ensure they are kept clear and accessible at all times.
Different fires require different extinguishers — know which type to use for electrical, chemical, or general fires.
If you discover a fire, sound the alarm and alert others without delay before attempting to tackle it.
Personal safety comes first — if the fire is spreading or you feel unsafe, evacuate immediately and wait for emergency services.
