Lesson 3: Control Measures and the Hierarchy of Control

🚫
Eliminate the hazard
Remove the hazard completely from the workplace if possible — this is the most effective control measure.
1
🔄
Substitute with something safer
If elimination isn't possible, replace the hazard with a safer alternative (e.g., use a less harmful substance or quieter machine).
2
🛡️
Implement engineering controls
Use physical measures like guards, barriers, or ventilation systems to separate people from hazards.
3
📋
Apply administrative controls
Change how work is organised by introducing procedures, signage, or training to reduce exposure to risks.
4
🦺
Use personal protective equipment (PPE)
When risks can't be fully controlled, provide suitable PPE such as gloves, helmets, or safety goggles as a final line of defence.
5
🎉 Excellent! You've explored the complete hierarchy of control measures. Remember: start at the top (elimination) and work your way down for maximum safety effectiveness!
