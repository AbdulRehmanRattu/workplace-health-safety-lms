Module 6: Building a Safe and Healthy Work Environment

Lesson 3: Embedding Safety into Everyday Behaviour

✅
How can you make safety part of daily routines?
Skip safety checks to save time
Integrate safety checks into everyday tasks
Only think about safety during emergencies
Leave safety to management
✅
Correct!
Making safety part of daily routines: Integrate safety checks and risk awareness into everyday tasks so that safe working becomes second nature.
👨‍💼
What is the best way for supervisors to promote safety?
Only give verbal instructions
Consistently model safe behaviour
Ignore minor safety issues
Focus only on productivity
✅
Correct!
Leading by example: Supervisors and senior staff should consistently model safe behaviour to encourage others to do the same.
🏆
Why is it important to recognise safe actions?
It wastes time and resources
It reinforces positive behaviour and motivation
It's not necessary
It creates competition
✅
Correct!
Recognising and rewarding safe behaviour: Acknowledging individuals or teams who prioritise safety reinforces positive behaviour and motivation.
👤
What role does personal responsibility play in safety?
Only managers are responsible
Everyone has a role in maintaining safety
Safety is optional
Only trained staff need to worry
✅
Correct!
Encouraging personal responsibility: Everyone has a role to play in maintaining safety — employees should stay alert, report issues, and act responsibly.
🔄
How often should safety be reinforced?
Once a year
Only during incidents
Frequently and regularly
Never needed
✅
Correct!
Reviewing and reinforcing regularly: Frequent communication, reminders, and refresher training help sustain a culture where safety is always prioritised.
