Module 6: Building a Safe and Healthy Work Environment

Lesson 1: Promoting Wellbeing and Mental Health at Work

Recognising the importance of wellbeing

A healthy workforce is more productive, engaged, and resilient. Supporting both physical and mental health benefits everyone in the organisation.

Reducing stress and workload pressure

Encourage open conversations about workload, set realistic expectations, and ensure employees take regular breaks to prevent burnout.

Creating a supportive environment

Promote inclusion, respect, and teamwork so staff feel valued and confident to raise wellbeing concerns.

Providing access to support

Offer access to wellbeing resources such as employee assistance programmes, counselling, or mental health first aiders.

Encouraging work-life balance

Support flexible working where possible and promote time off to maintain energy, motivation, and overall mental health.
